# Business Model & Regulatory Compliance Guide

## 1. Monetization Models

### Model A: Licensed Insurance Web Aggregator (Consumer B2C)
- Subject to IRDAI (Insurance Web Aggregator) Regulations.
- Revenue: Lead generation and solicitation commissions as capped by statutory guidelines.
- Requirement: Zero bias in comparison screens; no sponsor endorsements or misleading "best plan" labels.

### Model B: Enterprise B2B Document Intelligence API (SaaS)
- Sell policy extraction, comparison, and change-detection APIs to:
  - Insurance Brokers & Corporate HR Benefits Platforms.
  - Wealth tech platforms & Neo-banks offering insurance advisory.
  - InsurTech startups requiring normalized policy schemas.

### Model C: "Policy Health Check" & Renewal Audits
- Consumers upload their existing renewal notices or policy PDFs.
- AI extracts hidden sub-limits, changes between Version 1 and Version 2, and room rent caps.
